# Privacy Policy for Ludo & Multi Casual Games Bundle

**Last Updated:** November 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application **Ludo & Multi Casual Games Bundle** (herein referred to as the "App").

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use
Our App is designed to provide entertaining offline and casual board games (Ludo, Snakes & Ladders, and Tic-Tac-Toe). We do **not** collect, store, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or contacts.

All game progress, scores, and preferences (such as sound on/off) are stored locally on your device using local device storage (`SharedPreferences`).

---

### 2. Advertising and Third-Party SDKs
To keep this App free to play, we utilize **Google AdMob** (a service provided by Google LLC) to serve advertisements:
- **Banner Ads:** Displayed at the bottom of the screen.
- **Interstitial Ads:** Displayed strictly when a match ends (Victory Screen). We enforce a strict policy where no ads interrupt active gameplay.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process pseudonymous data in accordance with their privacy practices, including:
- Advertising ID (Google Play Services advertising ID).
- Device identifiers (IP address, operating system version, device model).
- Ad interaction statistics (impressions, clicks).

For more details on how Google uses data when you use our App, please refer to:
[Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

---

### 3. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)
Our App is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. All ads shown in the app comply with Google Play's Families Policy and Families Self-Certified Ads SDK requirements.

---

### 4. Permissions Used
The App requires the following standard Android permissions:
- `android.permission.INTERNET`: Required by Google AdMob to load advertisements.
- `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Used to check internet availability before loading ads.
- `com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID`: Used by Google Mobile Ads SDK on Android 13+ devices to serve relevant advertising.

---

### 5. Security
The security of your device and data is important to us. Because we do not transmit personal data to external private servers, your gameplay data remains safe on your local device.

---

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. You are advised to review this page occasionally for any changes.
